The rate at which the US markets have changed (mutated) since COVID is nuts. Before COVID, the last major "innovation", if you want to call it that, was 0-day options. But since then, you've had crypto trading, and with that, stablecoins, derivatives, NFTs, and DeFi, not to forget shitcoins.

For all the jokes and memes, crypto is fully mainstream now, with almost full regulatory blessing, with futures, ETFs, etc.

Then you have prediction markets. You can call it betting or gambling, but they've also become mainstream, so much so that stockbrokers like Robinhood now get a big chunk of their revenue from them.

But putting my thinking hat on, the one good side effect of all that speculative craziness is that it gets people into the market. Nothing expands the markets like greed, FOMO, and craziness. Nobody ever read Morgan Housel and started investing. Dogecoin, on the other hand, has done more for financial literacy than anything else. On the flip side, such easy access to speculation is bad for people and society.
