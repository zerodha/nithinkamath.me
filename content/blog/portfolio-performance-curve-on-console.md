Looking at the absolute returns of your portfolio doesn't tell you much. You need to compare it to a reasonable benchmark to know how well you have done. You can do this with the [portfolio performance curve on Console.](https://console.zerodha.com/dashboard)

If you've beaten your benchmark, then good. If not, you're probably better off investing in some index funds. The only issue is you can't flaunt your index fund investments 😬
