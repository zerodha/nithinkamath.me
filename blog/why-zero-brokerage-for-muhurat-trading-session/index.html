Why zero brokerage for Muhurat trading session?

08 Nov 2026

Someone on the team asked me the story behind why brokerage is free for all trades during the Diwali Muhurat trading session.

Honestly, there isn’t a backstory. We kept brokerage free as a way of saying thank you to all the customers for trading with someone like us, who was very new at the time. And in the 16 years since, we’ve kept it the same for every Muhurat session.🪔
