Introducing Trading from Chart (TFC) on Kite app

09 Sep 2026

Around 80% of our orders come from mobile. Personally, I still don’t understand how people manage to trade intraday from a phone. 🙂

That said, Trade from Charts (TFC) on mobile has been one of the most requested features. It’s now available on TradingView charts on Kite.

With TFC, you can place orders, drag to modify pending orders, and track open positions directly on the chart.
