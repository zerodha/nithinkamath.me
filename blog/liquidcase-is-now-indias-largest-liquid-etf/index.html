LIQUIDCASE is now India’s largest liquid ETF

18 Nov 2026

It’s been nearly 3 years since we launched LIQUIDCASE, and it is now the largest liquid ETF in India, with nearly ₹12,000 crore in AUM.

We built it to fix one problem. Other liquid ETFs paid out returns as tiny dividends every day, either in cash or as additional units, and both were a pain to deal with. First, you had to keep track of all the dividends to calculate your returns, and then filing taxes on top of this was a nightmare.

With LIQUIDCASE, you don’t have to deal with any of these hassles. It works just like any other growth scheme. All the returns are added to the NAV. You buy at X and sell at Y; the difference is your return, and you pay tax only when you sell.

It’s also why a lot of traders use it for margin. You pledge it once, and the pledged value keeps growing with the NAV. With other liquid ETFs, you have to pledge the new units every day.

Kudos to Vishal Jain and the entire Zerodha Case Funds team 🙂
