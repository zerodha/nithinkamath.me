When do traders use Kill Switch the most?

26 Nov 2026

We were looking at when traders use Kill Switch the most. Kill Switch is a feature we offer to disable a specific trading segment or take a break from trading.

I would’ve guessed that Kill Switch requests would go up when markets fall, but that doesn’t seem to be the case. Direction barely matters, what matters is volatility.

Across millions of Kill Switch requests this year, our busiest week was during an 8.6% Nifty rally.

It looks like overtrading isn’t a bear-market problem. It’s a volatility problem.

Btw, Kill Switch will soon be available on Kite. We’re also adding more controls, like the ability to set a daily limit on the number of orders you can place, etc.
