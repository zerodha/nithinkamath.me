IPOs are the only bullish corner of the market right now

07 Oct 2026

The only bullish corner of the market right now is IPOs.

The broader market has been steadily falling. The numbers may not fully show it, but it certainly feels like a bear market. IPOs, on the other hand, are a full-on party. Most recent listings have done well, and you can see it in the rush of activity.

I don’t remember the last time we saw such a stark divergence between the primary and secondary markets.

Strange are the times we live in.🙂
